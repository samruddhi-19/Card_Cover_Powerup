Picking one

What actually separates them

All four show the same real data and apply covers the same way. The difference is what you look at while deciding:

  • A shows one cover on a real card, in detail, while you browse. Best if choosing well matters more than choosing fast.
  • B shows thirty covers on real cards at once. Best for gradients, where a name tells you nothing.
  • C shows one cover big and cinematic. Best if this is a feature you want to show off.
  • D shows no cover at all until you land on it. Best only if people already know what they want.

A and B combine cleanly — the gallery becomes the Gradient tab inside the split layout. That is where I would take it unless you prefer one whole.
